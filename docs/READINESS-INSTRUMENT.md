## What these are

Trestle's own wording. Each item operationalises a construct from the readiness literature in
`RESEARCH.md` §2 and the ERP critical-success-factor studies in §1.2; the source column names the
construct, not a copied item. No published scale's item text is reproduced.

Before the capstone uses them as a measure, they need the content-validity step in `RESEARCH.md`
§6.3(c): an expert panel rates each item's relevance (CVI), then Cronbach's alpha on pilot data.

Scale: 5-point agreement (Strongly disagree … Strongly agree). Items marked **R** are
reverse-worded and reverse-scored; they also catch straight-lining.

## The default instrument

### Why we are changing

These questions are about the new system and the reasons for it.

| # | Item | Dimension | R | Construct and source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I understand why the organisation is going ahead with the new system. | Awareness & Understanding |  | Discrepancy (Armenakis et al. 1993) |

## Open questions for review

1. **Personal valence** ("what happens to me?") has no dimension. `RESEARCH.md` §2.5 and §8 item 1
   lean towards adding it as a seventh dimension; it is the belief that most directly predicts
   individual resistance, and it matters most for automation engagements.
2. **Business process readiness** is in the ODTR framework (`POSITIONING.md` §5.2) but not here.
3. Wording is international English; check it reads naturally to the first clients' staff.
